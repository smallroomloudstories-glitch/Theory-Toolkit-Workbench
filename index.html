Theory Toolkit — WORKBENCH

Experimental development version.

Theory Toolkit is a visual companion for music students and instructors. It is designed to make musical relationships easier to see during a lesson and easier to revisit while practicing or reviewing.

Best viewed on a laptop or desktop: The illustrated fretboard Explorers are not yet optimized for phones, where notes may be missing or misaligned.

How to help test Theory Toolkit

Please explore rather than following a strict test script. Change selections, try combinations, and see whether each page behaves the way you expect it to.

The feedback I am most interested in is:

  • What worked? What felt clear, useful, or intuitive?
  • What did not? Did anything behave incorrectly, confusingly, or awkwardly?
  • What did you expect? If something surprised you, what did you think would happen instead?
  • What would you like to see added? Is there a control, comparison, view, or piece of information that would make this more useful?

You do not need to be a music-theory expert to give useful feedback. If something does not make sense, that itself is useful information. Instructors and experienced players are also encouraged to identify anything musically misleading or anything they would present differently to a student.

Theory Toolkit is a teaching supplement. It does not replace an instructor or independently teach music theory.
